Seven critters, one song

Each critter plays one part of the tune, but only while it's in the box.

  • Touch a critter with your cursor to pick it up.
  • Carry it into the box to add its sound.
  • When one starts shaking, hover over it to calm it down, or it escapes.
  • Get all seven in at once and keep the full band playing.
