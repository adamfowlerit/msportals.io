Critter Sounds

Catch critters, make a song

Every critter sings or plays its own part, but only while it's in the box.

  • Touch a critter to grab it, then carry it into the box. Click (or press Esc) to let go.
  • Wild critters wander in. Box them to catch them before they run off. Some are rare, and a few are shiny!
  • Catch the same critter again and again to make it evolve.
  • Every day of the month has its own critter of the day. Can you catch all 31?
  • Fill all 7 spots and press FUSE! to merge them into one super critter.
  • Find secret combos, finish missions and fill your sticker book.
